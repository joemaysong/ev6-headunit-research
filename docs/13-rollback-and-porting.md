# Retention and recovery

Keep the first pristine original backup and this exact final release. Verify
all final and original files against the private manifests. The initial full
backup may be on offline external storage; keep its original path/manifest.
Intermediate candidate builds and diagnostic captures have been retired.

The private final runtime provides bit-exact restoration. Rebuilds are new
artifacts until separately verified. Retain the original local package signing
identity privately for update compatibility. Never publish firmware or keys.
Before changing runtime, use originalUI manualstop and verify native/backend/
originalassociation inactive andAPoff. Preserve any working session. Restore only
known exact files, originalpermissions and read-onlysystem, then verify startup.
Do not apply a profile to another firmware without resolving its own contracts.
